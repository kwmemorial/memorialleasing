About us

A Houston residential property management company.

WHO WE ARE

Houston homes, looked after as if they were our own.

Memorial Leasing & Management manages single-family homes, townhomes and condos for owners across the Houston area, from inside the Loop to the outer suburbs. We find and screen residents, collect rent, coordinate maintenance and keep owners informed with clear monthly statements. Our owners bring together Houston brokerage leadership and hands-on Texas property management experience, backed by KW Memorial, which has served Houston and its suburbs since its founding in 1989.

OWNERSHIP

The people behind the company

Michael Bossart

OWNER · BROKER

Michael is the operating principal and broker of record for Keller Williams Memorial. Since taking the helm in 2008, when the office had 151 agents, he has grown it into one of Houston’s largest brokerages. He was named Houston Agent Magazine’s 2019 Managing Broker of the Year, and earlier led Fidelity National Title’s San Antonio division. As broker for Memorial Leasing & Management, he oversees licensing, policies and every owner’s trust funds.

Roger Aad

OWNER

Roger is president and team leader of Keller Williams Memorial, where he leads the office’s operations and staff and consults with top-producing agents on growing their productivity and profitability. He is also a luxury agent with the Certified Luxury Home Marketing Specialist (CLHMS) and GUILD designations, holds the Certified International Property Specialist (CIPS) designation and speaks Arabic and French, so he knows the high-end homes and international clients many of our owners work with. He brings that same focus on service and systems to Memorial Leasing & Management, and he is the first person owners and agents talk to as the company grows.

CO

Craig Owen

OWNER

Craig is operating principal for Keller Williams in San Antonio and has co-owned the San Antonio franchise since 2001. Before moving into leadership he sold more than 1,200 homes, and he has trained thousands of real estate professionals as a former Keller Williams University faculty member. He brings years of experience running a property management operation, and the systems behind it, to Memorial Leasing & Management. He holds the GRI, CRS and ABR designations.
