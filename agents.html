Questions agents ask

Who can refer an owner?

How much is the referral fee?

How is the referral fee paid?

Will you try to sell my client's home?

No. We specialize in property management. When your client decides to sell, we send them back to you.

Do I have to refer my lease listings to you?

No. Every KW Memorial agent, including our luxury and commercial agents, can work a lease listing themselves. Refer it to us when you would rather we handle it.

What kinds of properties do you manage?

Single-family homes, townhouses, condominiums, duplexes and fourplexes across Houston and its suburbs. Own an apartment building or commercial property? We can introduce you to KW Commercial Texas.

What happens after I submit a referral?

Someone from our team will contact your client, and we will keep you updated as the property comes under management.
