QUESTIONS

Is there a minimum rent for luxury marketing?

No. High-end homes get luxury marketing materials as part of our standard service, with the same team and the same process as every home we lease.

Who leases executive homes in Houston?

Many renters are professionals relocating for work with Houston’s energy, medical and corporate employers who lease while they get to know the city.

Do you work with the KW Memorial Luxury Division?

Yes. We are backed by KW Memorial, and its luxury agents can lease a home themselves or refer it to us. When you are ready to sell, you go back to your agent.

Which neighborhoods do you serve?

River Oaks, the Memorial Villages, West University Place, Tanglewood, Memorial and high-end homes across Houston and its suburbs.

How do I get started?

Request a free rental analysis and someone from our team will reach out shortly.
