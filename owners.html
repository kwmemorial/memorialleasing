GETTING YOUR HOME RENT-READY

A rent-ready home leases faster.

Before we market your home, we walk it with you and recommend any work that will help it lease quickly and for the right rent.

  • Clean throughout, including appliances, floors and windows
  • Fresh paint or touch-ups where walls are marked
  • All appliances, plumbing, heating and air conditioning working
  • Working smoke alarms and light fixtures
  • Yard trimmed and entry tidy, since the front door is the first photo
  • Locks re-keyed between residents
