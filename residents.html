RESIDENT FAQ

Questions residents ask

What are the rental requirements?

Each adult 18 or older applies separately. You need a credit score of at least 600, income of at least three times the monthly rent and two months of pay stubs. We also verify rental history and employment and run a background check. [Pending confirmation]

How much is the security deposit?

Usually one month's rent, plus a $350 non-refundable cleaning deposit and a $100 non-refundable move-in fee. [Pending confirmation]

Are pets allowed?

That depends on the home. Where pets are allowed, every pet is screened through Petscreening, with a $300 non-refundable pet fee and $35 a month pet rent per pet. [Pending confirmation]

What about service and assistance animals?

Service and assistance animals are not pets, so no pet fees or pet rent apply. Submit your request through Petscreening and we will follow up.

When is rent due?

Rent is due on the 1st of the month and is paid online through the resident portal. A late fee applies if rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the 3rd. [Pending confirmation]

Who pays utilities?

Residents set up and pay all utilities unless the lease says otherwise.

How much notice do I give before moving out?

Sixty days' written notice, including on a month-to-month lease. [Pending confirmation]

When will I get my deposit back?

Within 30 days after you move out and return the keys, with an itemized list of any deductions, as Texas law requires.

Thinking about buying someday?

Many residents rent while they learn a neighborhood. When you are ready to buy, we will introduce you to a KW Memorial agent who knows the area.

Relocating to Houston from abroad?

Many of our residents move here for work with international companies, and many rent first and buy once they know the city. KW Memorial’s International Division, with agents who speak more than 40 languages, works with families relocating to Houston.
